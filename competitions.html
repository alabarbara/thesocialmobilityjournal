As part of our mission to assist homeless youth in securing long-term mental and socioeconomic health, SMJ leads a virtual mentorship program every summer in partnership with local homelessness service providers. Mentors help youth design a 6-month, 1-year, and 3-year plan for income and housing security. Mentorship for many youth can be a life-line in times of crisis.
